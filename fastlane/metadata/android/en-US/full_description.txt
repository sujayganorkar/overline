Overline is the modern, privacy-first PDF reader, editor, and signer engineered for speed and uncompromising data sovereignty. Powered by a high-performance native Rust core, Overline handles large documents, complex forms, and multi-page annotations entirely on your device—with zero cloud uploads, zero tracking, and no subscriptions.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
• Your files never leave your device. All rendering, editing, and saving happen locally in sandboxed memory.
• No account registration, no telemetry spying, no third-party cloud uploads.
• Digital signatures are stored encrypted at rest on your local device.

⚡ BLAZING-FAST NATIVE PERFORMANCE
• Engineered with a native Rust core engine for instant page rendering and butter-smooth zooming.
• Memory-optimized LRU page streaming ensures smooth scrolling even on massive, image-heavy documents.
• Native 64-bit architecture built for modern Android devices.

✍️ ANNOTATE, MARKUP & COMMENT
• Highlight, underline, strikethrough, and add colorful freehand ink notes.
• Add threaded page comments with status tracking (open/resolved).
• Insert shapes, text boxes, and stamps directly onto the page.

📝 FILL & SIGN FORMS
• Draw smooth, pressure-sensitive signatures or import signature images.
• Reuse saved signatures securely across contracts, agreements, and tax documents.
• Flatten annotations and signatures before sharing to prevent tampering.

🛠️ POWERFUL PDF TOOLBOX
• Merge & Split: Combine multiple PDFs or extract selected page ranges into fresh documents.
• Page Organizer: Reorder, rotate, and delete pages with an intuitive visual grid.
• Target File Size Compression: Compress oversized PDFs to exact target file sizes (e.g. under 2MB for portal uploads).
• Convert Images to PDF: Snap photos of documents and convert them into crisp, standardized PDFs.
• On-Device OCR: Extract text from scanned documents locally.

📖 DESIGNED FOR READING COMFORT
• Material Design 3 interface with dynamic theming.
• System, Light, Dark, and High-Contrast Inverted reading modes.
• Horizontal and Vertical reading modes with quick jump sliders.
• Transparent in-app diagnostics and system health monitor.

Overline gives you complete control over your PDF workflow without sacrificing privacy.
